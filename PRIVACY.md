# Privacy policy

A French translation of this policy is available in
[PRIVACY.fr.md](PRIVACY.fr.md).

---

## In one sentence

**This application collects nothing, sends nothing, and sets no cookie.**
Everything you make stays in your browser, on your device.

---

## No data leaves your device

The application runs **entirely in your browser**. There is no application
server, no account, no sign-up.

- **No network request** goes out to any third-party service: no analytics, no
  remote font, no CDN, no social button. The only files loaded are the
  application's own, from the site hosting it.
- **No cookie** is set.
- **No personal data** is asked for, collected or transmitted.

## What is stored on your device

So that you find your work again from one visit to the next, the application
uses your browser's **local storage**. It stays on your device and is never
transmitted.

| Key | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `pm.paint.v1` | the drawing in progress and the settings |
| `pm.projects`, `pm.proj.*` | the projects you save yourself |
| `pm.restore` | the automatic restore points |

This information is **technical and creative** in nature: notes, instrument
settings, a tempo. It cannot identify you.

**To erase all of it**: clear the site data for this page in your browser's
settings. The application also lets you delete projects and restore points one
by one.

## Offline use

The application can be installed and used without a connection. A *service
worker* keeps a copy of its files (pages, scripts, styles, icons) in your
browser's cache. It stores nothing about you.

## Sound and MIDI

Sound is **computed by your browser**; no audio recording is uploaded or
downloaded.

If you turn MIDI on, the browser first asks for your explicit permission. The
application then reads the **names of your MIDI devices** so it can offer them
in a list, and exchanges notes with them. That information stays local and is
neither stored nor transmitted. The permission can be revoked at any time in
your browser's settings.

## Exports

WAV, MIDI and `.json` files are **produced in your browser** and then saved by
it to your downloads folder. They pass through no server.

## Your rights

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you rights of access,
rectification and erasure over your personal data. As this application
**processes no personal data** and keeps none off your device, you exercise
those rights directly yourself: clearing your browser's storage deletes
everything.

For any question: *contact address to be filled in*.

## Children

The application asks for no information and is suitable for all ages. No
child's data is collected, since no data is.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page, with its date.

## A note on cookie banners

The application **uses no cookies** and puts in local storage only what is
**strictly necessary** for the service you explicitly asked for: keeping your
work. Storage of that kind is generally exempt from prior consent.

That holds for the application alone. **If the site hosting it also runs
analytics, advertising or third-party content, its own obligations apply** and
are unchanged.
